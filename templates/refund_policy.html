1. Scope

This policy explains cancellation and refund requests for paid Ennoic plans. The amount, currency, renewal interval, taxes, and payment provider are shown in checkout before you confirm a purchase. Ennoic does not currently have a Paddle checkout integration in the application.

2. Recurring Pro subscriptions and cancellation

Where offered, Pro is a monthly recurring subscription. It renews automatically at the price shown in checkout until canceled. Canceling stops future renewals; it does not automatically refund a charge already made. Unless the checkout provider or applicable law provides otherwise, access remains available through the paid billing period and then returns to the Free plan.

If Paddle processes your order, use the “Manage subscription” link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle Buyer Support to cancel. Paddle says cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current billing period and prevents further payments. If another provider processes the order, follow that provider's cancellation instructions or email ennoic.app@gmail.com for help.

3. Requesting a refund

For Paddle-processed transactions: Paddle is the Merchant of Record and handles buyer payment support and refund requests. Submit a request using the support link in your receipt or through Paddle.net. Paddle reviews requests under its current Buyer Refund Policy, Buyer Terms, and applicable law. Paddle's policy says transactions are generally non-refundable unless a legal right applies, while Paddle may also grant discretionary refunds; the policy varies by jurisdiction and transaction. This page does not promise that a request will be approved.

For transactions processed by another provider: contact ennoic.app@gmail.com with the account email, transaction date, and payment reference. We will review the request with the applicable provider. No refund is promised by this policy; any decision remains subject to the provider's process and applicable law.

4. Consumer rights

This policy does not remove or restrict consumer rights that cannot legally be waived. Rules on withdrawal periods, digital services, subscription renewals, and refunds vary by country. Where applicable law gives you a right that is more protective than this policy, that law applies.

5. Contact and details to confirm

For help, email ennoic.app@gmail.com. Paddle's seller guidance asks sellers to provide buyer support contact details, including a phone number, and to use the Paddle Buyer Support route for Paddle orders. Ennoic has not supplied a public support phone number, and Paddle is not currently integrated in this application. Confirm these operational details before Paddle review or enabling Paddle checkout.
