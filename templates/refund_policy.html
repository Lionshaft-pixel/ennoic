1. Subscription charges

When Pro checkout is available, the subscription renews monthly until cancelled. The price, currency, billing interval, and any applicable taxes are shown before you confirm the purchase. Review the current pricing page and checkout details before subscribing.

2. Cancelling a subscription

Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not itself refund a previous charge. For an order where Paddle is named as the reseller, use the subscription-management link in your Paddle receipt or customer portal, or visit Paddle Buyer Support. A cancellation scheduled for the end of a billing period ordinarily leaves access available through the period already paid for.

If your receipt identifies a different payment provider, follow the cancellation instructions supplied by that provider for your order.

3. Requesting a refund

For Paddle-processed orders, submit a refund request through the support link in your receipt or through Paddle Buyer Support. Paddle's Buyer Refund Policy describes how it reviews requests. Refund eligibility depends on that policy and any consumer rights that apply to your purchase; a refund is not guaranteed by this Ennoic policy.

For help with access or a technical issue, contact Ennoic at ennoic.app@gmail.com. If your receipt identifies another payment provider, use that provider's refund instructions for the transaction.

4. Applicable rights

This policy does not remove or restrict any cancellation or refund rights that cannot legally be waived in your location. The payment provider may explain the process for exercising those rights for an order it processed. For Paddle purchases, see the Paddle Buyer Terms and refund policy.
