1. Information Ennoic collects and stores

  • Account and sign-in information: email address, password hash (not plaintext password), email-verification status, account creation and password-change dates, and sign-in/session state. Ennoic stores hashed one-time email-verification and password-reset tokens while they are needed.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose Google, Google processes the authentication request and returns verified identity information. Ennoic uses your verified email address for the account; it does not receive your Google password.
  • Security and service activity: the most recent account login time and IP address; an IP address and timestamps associated with an account deletion request; and request metadata in application and hosting logs, which may include IP address, URL, time, and browser user agent.
  • Content: task titles and details, conversation titles and messages, pinned status, saved-memory details (person, fact or item, and time), and task proposals created during conversations.
  • Usage, subscription, and checkout details: AI request counts, identifiers, status, request hashes, and cached response data used to safely handle retries; plan and subscription status, billing-period dates, provider subscription identifiers, and payment-event identifiers. If you choose to save checkout contact details, Ennoic stores your name, email, and phone number.
  • Messages to support: information you include when contacting ennoic.app@gmail.com.

Ennoic does not currently offer file uploads or attachment processing.

2. AI processing

Ennoic uses Groq's API to classify some messages and produce AI answers. Depending on the request, Groq may receive the message you submit, relevant recent conversation messages, saved-memory context, and instructions needed to process the request. Groq processes that information under its own terms and privacy practices. Review Groq's Privacy Policy before submitting information you consider sensitive.

Some task actions are handled by Ennoic's application logic without sending a request to an AI model. AI output may be inaccurate; check it before relying on it or confirming a suggested action.

3. Service providers and sharing

Ennoic shares information with providers as needed to operate the features you use:

  • Google: handles sign-in when you choose Continue with Google. Ennoic receives your verified email address.
  • Groq: receives messages and relevant context for AI processing, as described above.
  • Discord (when notifications are configured): Ennoic sends the signed-in account's email address, the message submitted to the chat, and Ennoic's reply to the Discord channel associated with the configured webhook for operational monitoring. Channel members can view these notifications. Discord processes and retains them under its own policies; Ennoic has not confirmed a retention period for Discord messages. Do not submit information you do not want shared with that channel.
  • Render: hosts the Ennoic web application and may process service and request information in that role. See Render's Privacy Policy.
  • Neon: provides the production PostgreSQL database used for account and application records. See Neon's parent company's Privacy Notice.
  • Gmail SMTP: sends account-verification and password-reset emails in the reviewed configuration. Google processes the recipient address and message needed for delivery. The application can use another configured email provider if the deployment changes.
  • Paddle: Paddle is the intended Merchant of Record for a future Paddle checkout. It is not currently processing Ennoic payments. If a future checkout identifies Paddle as reseller, Paddle will process buyer payment and transaction information under its own terms and privacy notices.

The Ennoic application stores subscription status and provider identifiers and may store checkout contact details when you ask it to save them. It does not store full payment-card numbers. Ennoic does not sell your task, conversation, or saved-memory content.

4. Cookies, sessions, and currency display

Ennoic uses a session cookie for sign-in and account state, and session data for security checks such as cross-site request protection and temporary Google sign-in state. The pricing page may use a country header or browser language to choose a displayed currency; that selection is not stored as a separate account profile field.

5. Retention and account deletion

You can request account deletion in settings. The account enters a 30-day recovery period; signing in again within that period cancels the request and restores access. After the recovery period, account data is scheduled for deletion by the application. A limited deletion record containing a hashed account reference, email address, request IP address, and relevant dates is retained for 90 days after account deletion or cancellation of the request. Processing may be delayed if the service is unavailable.

Email, hosting, AI, payment, and backup providers may retain information under their own policies. Ennoic has not confirmed a single retention period across those providers and does not promise a fixed period for their logs or backups. For privacy questions, contact ennoic.app@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and contact

You can view or manage saved memories, conversations, and tasks in the application, and request account deletion through settings. You can contact ennoic.app@gmail.com with questions or requests concerning information associated with your account. Rights available to you depend on applicable law.
