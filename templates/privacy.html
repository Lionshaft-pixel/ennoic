1. Information Ennoic handles

  • Account information: email address, password hash (not your plaintext password), email-verification status, account creation and password-change timestamps, and authentication version. Hashed one-time email-verification and password-reset tokens are stored while needed. Google sign-in supplies your verified email; Ennoic does not store a Google profile photo in the account record.
  • Account and security activity: the most recent login time and IP address, and the IP address and request details recorded when an account deletion is requested. Temporary Google sign-in state is held in the session while that sign-in is completed. Standard hosting and application logs may also record request metadata such as IP address, route, time, and browser user agent.
  • Content you enter or create: task titles and details, conversation titles and messages, pinned status, saved-memory fields (person, fact/object, and time), and task proposals generated during conversations.
  • Usage and subscription records: AI-request counts, request identifiers and status, a hash derived from the request, and a cached response payload used for safe retries; plan and subscription status; provider subscription identifiers and billing-period dates; and processed payment-event identifiers. The database schema also supports checkout contact details (name, email, phone) if you choose to save them.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when emailing ennoic.app@gmail.com.

File attachments are not currently available in the application. The current plan configuration reserves attachment limits, but Ennoic does not currently provide an attachment-upload feature.

2. How AI processing works

Ennoic uses Groq's API to classify some messages and generate AI responses. Depending on the request, Groq may receive the message you send, recent messages from that conversation, Ennoic instructions, and relevant saved-memory context. Ennoic sends this content to Groq for processing; Groq handles it under its own terms and privacy practices. Ennoic does not control Groq's independent retention or use practices, so review Groq's current Privacy Policy before submitting sensitive information.

Some task-management requests may be handled by Ennoic's application logic without an AI request. AI responses can be inaccurate; verify important information and review suggested actions.

3. Service providers and sharing

Ennoic uses or may use the following providers to operate the features you request:

  • Google: verifies your identity when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Groq: processes the message and relevant context for AI features.
  • Database and hosting providers: store and serve account records and application data. The app uses PostgreSQL when a DATABASE_URL is configured and SQLite for local development.
  • Resend or an SMTP provider: delivers account verification and password-reset emails, if email sending is configured.
  • Payment provider: receives information needed to process a purchase and manage a subscription when checkout is enabled. The current code has a Razorpay adapter; Paddle is not currently integrated. If Paddle is added and is named as the reseller at checkout, Paddle acts as Merchant of Record for those Paddle transactions and processes buyer payment/support information under its own notices and terms.

Ennoic does not sell account or conversation content. Information may also be disclosed when necessary to respond to lawful requests, protect the Service or people from harm, or address fraud or abuse, subject to applicable law.

4. Cookies and location

The application uses session and security-related browser state to sign you in and protect account actions. The pricing page may use a request country header or browser language to select a displayed currency; this choice is not described in the application code as a separate profile field.

5. Retention and deletion

Account data is stored while the account is active. If you request deletion in settings, Ennoic immediately marks the account for deletion and schedules an application purge 30 days after the request. Signing in again within that 30-day period cancels the deletion request and restores account access. The current deletion process keeps a minimal audit record containing a hashed account reference, email address, request time, IP address, and deletion-status dates. Its expiry is set for 90 days after the account purge (or after a deletion cancellation, if you cancel the request); cleanup runs through the background deletion worker.

Payment-provider records, email-provider records, infrastructure logs, and backups may be subject to those providers' own retention schedules. Ennoic has not established or verified a single retention period for those systems, so no fixed period is promised here. Email ennoic.app@gmail.com for privacy or deletion questions.

6. Your choices

You can manage saved memories, conversations, and tasks in the application and request deletion of your account in settings. You can also contact Ennoic at ennoic.app@gmail.com to ask about information associated with your account. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights under local privacy law; contact us and we will review your request under applicable requirements.

7. Details that need confirmation

Before relying on a deletion request for a production account, confirm that the production database applies the configured foreign-key cascades and that the background deletion worker is running. Ennoic has not established fixed retention periods for its hosting, email, AI, payment, and backup providers, so those periods are not stated here.
